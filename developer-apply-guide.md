# Developer Job Application Guide

## How to Apply to Jobs

The issue is that you have uploaded resumes but haven't applied to any jobs yet. Here's how to apply:

### Step 1: Login as a Developer
- Go to `http://localhost:3000`
- Login with your developer account

### Step 2: Navigate to Jobs
- Click on "Browse Jobs" in the navigation menu
- This will take you to the job listings page

### Step 3: Apply to a Job
- Find a job you want to apply for
- Click the "Apply" button next to the job
- Select one of your uploaded resumes from the dropdown
- Click "Submit Application"

### Step 4: Verify Application
- After applying, the company should be able to see your application
- The company can then go to "Job Applications" to see all applications
