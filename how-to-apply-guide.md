# How to Apply to Jobs - Developer Guide

## Current Situation:
- ✅ **5 resumes uploaded** by developer
- ✅ **4 jobs available** in the system
- ❌ **0 applications submitted** (this is the problem!)

## Step-by-Step Instructions:

### 1. Login as Developer
- Go to `http://localhost:3000`
- Login with your developer account

### 2. Navigate to Jobs
- Click on **"Browse Jobs"** in the navigation menu
- You should see 4 available jobs:
  - HR Manager
  - Developer
  - Software Developer
  - Frontend Developer

### 3. Apply to a Job
- Find a job you want to apply for
- Click the **"Apply"** button next to the job
- A form will appear with:
  - Resume dropdown (select one of your uploaded resumes)
  - Submit button

### 4. Submit Application
- Select one of your uploaded resumes from the dropdown
- Click **"Submit Application"**
- You should see a success message

### 5. Apply to More Jobs (Optional)
- Repeat steps 3-4 for other jobs
- You can apply to multiple jobs with different resumes

## After Applying:
- **Company users** will be able to see your applications
- **Company dashboard** will show your applications with resumes
- **Similarity matching** will work with your resume data

## Test the Complete Flow:
1. **Developer**: Apply to at least 2-3 jobs
2. **Company**: Login and go to "Job Applications"
3. **Company**: Click "Sort by Similarity" to see resume scores
